Lark Wilson

Creative Director / Comedian

Sophisticated, eloquent and absurd — all words that frequent Lark's vocabulary. Lark Wilson, once described as a "hidden gem" of the New Zealand comedy scene, is one of the country's most exciting and original comedic talents. She will charm you with her idiosyncratic presence and poignant punchlines, all while making a visual statement in her own unique style.
