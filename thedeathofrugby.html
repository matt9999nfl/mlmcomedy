The Death Of Rugby

by Matt Lilley

Date
17 Oct 2025
Time
6:30 PM
Venue
Good Times Comedy Club
Price
$20

Christchurch comedy legend Matt Lilley delivered the eulogy rugby never knew it needed. "The Death Of Rugby" was a hilarious yet heartfelt farewell to New Zealand's most sacred institution.

From the glory days of World Cup triumphs to the crushing defeats that broke a nation's heart, Matt mourned our beloved game with the perfect blend of irreverence and affection.
